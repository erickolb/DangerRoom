Hardlight for Android

The Android client is nicknamed Hardlight. It appears on your device as Danger Room and uses app ID net.chateaulore.hardlight.

Hardlight 0.1.2 · Android 9 or newer, on phones and tablets.

Download Android APK ↓Install with ObtainiumRelease notes and checksum

If Obtainium is installed, the button adds the DangerRoom GitHub repository for Hardlight updates. Hardlight 0.1.2 is a normal release; prerelease updates are optional. Otherwise, install Obtainium first, then return here.

The launcher icon now matches the loading screen: a red background, thin dark outline around the circular mask, and dark-red lower-right shadow.

The app starts with a centered outlined mask with a diagonal shadow on a matching red background, then opens Want List, My Collection, Search Metron, Scan Cover, Cover Slideshow, and Reading Log. Connect it to your own HTTPS DangerRoom instance using an administrator-issued device ID and secret. Installing the APK does not grant access.

Scan Cover now tries Metron’s exact artwork fingerprint after you crop the photo. OCR and barcodes are off by default; enable the previous lookup in Scan settings if wanted. Photos stay on the phone. Exact matching is experimental and may miss camera photos or variants. Requires DangerRoom β-022 or later.

Moving from the older net.chateaulore.dangerroom app? Hardlight installs alongside it. Enter your instance and device credentials in the new app; your server collection stays the same.

Get started

  1. Run a DangerRoom β-022 or later server, or ask your instance administrator for access.
  2. Install the APK, then enter the instance hostname and the device credentials issued for your phone or tablet.
  3. Tap Save and test connection. For future updates, install the signed APK over the existing app.
